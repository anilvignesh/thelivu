# 1. What Thelivu is

Thelivu publishes fact-checked stories about India, Kerala first, as short articles and vertical reels (Instagram and YouTube Shorts).
The name is Malayalam for "evidence". Everything is sourced; the stance is an openly stated view from the side of ordinary people, never
disguised neutrality.

## Two content lanes (both matter, Anil 2026-10-22)

| Lane | Input | What it produces |
|---|---|---|
| **News desk** | news feeds (The Hindu, India Today, Business Standard, Medianama, TNM and more), tips, curated channels | explainer articles, then a reel per article |
| **Public records** | CAG audit reports for 29 states and UTs, government datasets | grounded findings, cross-year/cross-state patterns, then audit reels |

Selection rule for both: **impact x under-coverage**. Under-coverage only ever selects what to look at. It never confirms anything.
Not chasing sensational breaking news is a decision (Anil 2026-10-22: "we dont have to run after the sensational news").

## Rules that do not bend

1. **Publishing is gated by code, not by a human tap** (since 2026-08-29). Gates that refuse: verification (KILL/HOLD/READY),
   self-talk detection, number containment (every figure in a reel must appear in the cited evidence), the 90 second reel ceiling,
   the duplicate-post guard. A failed gate never turns into a warning that ships anyway. Anil reads what shipped and can delete it.
2. **Three buckets, always**: Fact, Allegation, Inference. An allegation is spoken as one ("the agency alleges").
3. **One source proposes, the whole web disposes.** A claim's origin never counts toward its own verification.
4. **Synthesis leads are arithmetic, not stories.** A pattern found by the detectors is a reason to read, not something to publish.
5. **Silence is reported.** A source or job that stops producing must raise a visible signal; "0 results" must never look like "all quiet".
6. **Facts outrank reach.** Design never changes or crops a claim or number. A reel opens on its sharpest fact.
